Tech Support Ticketing System

A Google Apps Script support desk for recording requests, assigning work, tracking status changes, and preparing team updates.

ROLE
Application and workflow development
FORMAT
Apps Script web app
TOOLS
Apps Script, Google Sheets, JavaScript, Teams notification payloads
STATUS
Built project; private work data excluded

Why I built it

When requests arrive through scattered messages, the support team needs a shared view of what is open, who owns the next action, and which changes need to reach the team. I built a ticket record and queue so an issue can move through assignment, updates, and resolution with a history that explains what happened.

What I built

  • Ticket intake and queue views with request details, filters, comments, and role-based permissions.
  • Assignment, transfer, status, resolution, reopening, and escalation actions with a recorded change history.
  • An operations dashboard that calculates ticket volume, urgent open work, response and resolution time, and downtime from stored records.
  • A notification queue that prepares updates for Microsoft Teams and Power Automate, including duplicate checks and delivery-attempt logging in the source.

Why it works this way

Show ownership. Assignment and status are visible in the ticket record, so the next step is easier to find.

Keep a trail. Comments and status events preserve the context behind changes.

Make updates dependable. Queue handling separates a ticket change from the attempt to deliver a notification.

Operating procedure

  1. Record the request

    Capture the issue, requester context, priority, and details needed to start work.

  2. Assign and act

    Place the ticket with an owner, add updates, and change status as work progresses. Use escalation when the next action needs a different owner or urgency.

  3. Prepare team updates

    Review queued notification payloads and delivery attempts separately from the ticket change itself.

  4. Resolve and review

    Record the resolution or reopen the ticket when needed. Use the history and dashboard to inspect the queue.

Outcome and limits

The Apps Script source implements intake, assignment, status and escalation changes, history, dashboard calculations, and notification queue handling. The illustrated tickets are fictional. There is no verified public deployment or measured change in response time, resolution time, or workload.

How I would measure impact · illustrative example only

If a team measured the same type of request at 10 minutes to triage before a shared queue and 7 minutes afterward, the reduction would be 30%: (10 − 7) ÷ 10. Those times are invented to show the method. They are not a result from this project.
