BUILT PROTOTYPE / HARDWARE + RECORDS

RFID Attendance System

An Arduino and RFID prototype that captures a card identifier and timestamp, then records the attendance event in a central database.

ROLE
System development
FORMAT
Hardware and record workflow
TOOLS
Arduino, RFID, database
STATUS
Demonstrated prototype

The problem

Manual attendance entries can be slow to collect and difficult to consolidate. A reliable event record needs both an identified card and the time it was read.

What I built

  • An RFID reader and Arduino flow for reading a card identifier.
  • Timestamped attendance events stored in a central database.
  • A simple interface to support review of captured records.

Operating procedure

  1. Confirm the reader and database connection are ready before a session.
  2. Present a registered card to the reader and confirm the capture response.
  3. Review the recorded identifier and timestamp in the central record.
  4. Investigate missing or repeated taps before using the log as an attendance report.

This is a sensible deployment checklist. The earlier portfolio does not document the complete enrollment and exception-handling implementation.

How it helped

The prototype replaced a handwritten event with a timestamped record that can be retrieved centrally. Its behavior was shown in a live demonstration, but this page does not claim a measured reduction in administrative time or a production deployment.

Evidence and limits: The build and live demonstration are described in my earlier public portfolio. Hardware, database access, personal identifiers, and real attendance logs are not part of the website demo.
