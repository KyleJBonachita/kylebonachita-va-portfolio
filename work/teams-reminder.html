Morning Tech Team reminder

A scheduled Teams Adaptive Card reminder for daily tech-team logging, built in Power Automate.

ROLE
Flow design and setup
FORMAT
Scheduled team reminder
TOOLS
Power Automate, Microsoft Teams, Adaptive Cards
STATUS
Built workflow

The task

Give the tech team a consistent morning prompt to record daily work, delivered in the tool they already use for communication.

What I built

  • A Power Automate flow scheduled for 6:00 AM on weekdays.
  • An Adaptive Card reminder for daily tech-team work logging.
  • Delivery through Microsoft Teams.

What this demonstrates

This small workflow shows how a scheduled trigger, structured message, and team delivery can replace a manual reminder. The case study does not claim measured time savings or completed downstream reporting.

Current status: The scheduled reminder is built. A separate EOD collection workflow remains under development and is not part of this case study.
